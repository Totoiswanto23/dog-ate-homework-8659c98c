## The line

100 characters, one line. Pure dog logic: the worksheet told the dog to "chew over the problem", so the dog obeyed literally — twice — and would do it again before breakfast. No embarrassment, no apology, no stock line about the cat or the taste of paper: a dog who believes it did exactly what it was told, and is faintly proud of the thoroughness.
